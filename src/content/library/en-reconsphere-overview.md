## Product direction

ReconSphere is a planned reconciliation engine and financial observability platform. Its direction covers fund consistency checks, discrepancy discovery, traceability, and resolution workflows. It is a core Smartscity engineering product currently in design and architecture review.

## Current stage

There is no runnable reconciliation implementation or installable build available in this guide. A design description is not an execution result. This page provides an overview and a consultation path, without installation commands, API examples, measured capacity, or claims of completed customer integrations.

## Prepare a reconciliation discussion

Describe the two sides of the comparison, their authoritative data owners, the business key, time window, currency and amount semantics, and how late or corrected records should be handled. Explain which differences need investigation and who can confirm the expected business state. A redacted schema or small illustrative example can help define the problem after an appropriate sharing method is agreed.

Do not send account credentials or customer financial records in an initial inquiry. Begin with the scope and the decision you need to make. Product design and any future implementation or evaluation have separate acceptance conditions.
