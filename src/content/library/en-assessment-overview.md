## When to start

Use an initial reliability assessment when architecture, change records, and operating evidence are spread across teams, or when a critical business path needs a shared risk assessment. Typical questions concern retry amplification, rollback compatibility, and shared failover dependencies.

## How the work is organized

The customer identifies business goals, system owners, and the access and verification boundaries. Experts establish the assessment method, confirm business semantics, design consequential verification, and review important findings. An Agent can organize materials, relate evidence, propose checks and questions, and help assemble a traceable report. The checks and tools used depend on the agreed engagement and available access.

This is a guide to an assessment service. It does not offer a self-service scanner, downloadable Agent, CLI, or API. It does not claim that every candidate check is automated.

## What you receive

Agree the deliverable before starting: a scoped risk view, findings linked to evidence, coverage gaps, prioritized remediation, and the conditions needed to revisit the assessment. A finding distinguishes what was observed, what is inferred, and what still needs verification. Recommendations identify the affected business path and the next action.

## Decide whether it fits

A useful starting point is one critical operation and a concrete decision: for example, whether a planned rollback can safely resume unfinished orders. Provide a responsible contact and the available material types. If access or evidence is insufficient, the assessment records those limits instead of issuing an unsupported assurance. Continue to the preparation guide to open a discussion.
